Wendi Yan is an artist, writer and technologist. She uses CGI software to simulate alternative scientific progress through videos and games, and writes about the history and future of scientific discovery.

Wendi was an inaugural Steve Jobs Archive Fellow and received Grand Prix of the 6th VH Award by Hyundai Motor Group. She directs the Revival Fund, an experimental grant program restoring lost research.
